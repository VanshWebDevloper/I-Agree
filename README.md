<h1>I Agree</h1>

<h3>Nobody Reads the Terms.</h3>

I Agree is a fictional browser-based educational game about the dangers of blindly accepting terms, permissions, and suspicious software.

The game starts like an ordinary computer experience. You move to another city, live alone while your parents are away, and eventually discover a strange application that seems harmless at first.

But the more you interact with it, the more unsettling things become.

<h2>About the Game</h2>

The player encounters a fictional software store and downloads an application without carefully reading its terms.

At first, everything seems normal.

Then the application begins asking for permissions, accessing things it shouldn't, and using information against the player.

The goal isn't simply to "win."

The game is designed to teach players how to recognize suspicious behavior online and what they can do when something feels wrong.

<h2>Game Modes</h2>

<h4>Educational</h4>

A more guided experience.

Important moments include explanations and tips about:

- Suspicious permissions
- Reading terms before accepting them
- Protecting personal information
- Recognizing manipulation and scams
- Saving evidence
- Telling a trusted adult

<h4>Gamified</h4>

A more game-like experience with:

- Timers
- Evidence collection
- Decisions
- Different outcomes
- Lessons revealed at the end

<h2>Story</h2>

You recently moved to another city.

Your parents aren't with you.

You're living alone for now.

One ordinary evening, you open your computer...

Something is waiting.

<h2>Endings</h2>

The player's decisions can lead to different endings.

Possible outcomes include:

- Good Ending — You recognize the danger, preserve evidence, and seek help.
- Middle Ending — You remove the suspicious application but don't tell anyone.
- Bad Ending — You continue following the application's demands.
- Secret Ending — You discover something hidden by actually reading the terms.

Regardless of the ending, the game leads to an "If This Happened to You" section with practical safety guidance.

<h2>Purpose</h2>

This is a fictional project created for learning and awareness.

The game does not use real people's information, real private data, or real criminal instructions.

Its purpose is to make digital-safety lessons more memorable by putting them inside an interactive story.
